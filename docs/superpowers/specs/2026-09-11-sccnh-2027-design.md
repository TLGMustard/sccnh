# SCCNH 2027 Volunteer Platform Design

## Product

SCCNH needs one shared operating surface for its January 25–27, 2027 event. Volunteers must be able to understand the event, compare locations and roles, see current capacity, claim a shift, review their shifts, and cancel. Organizers must be able to view the roster, mark general and lead training, adjust safe capacities, and check attendance during the event.

## Experience

The public page is a compact working surface, not a marketing landing page. A short SCCNH explainer and event facts lead immediately into day controls and shift cards. Each shift exposes its time, role, remaining capacity, location context, and any special expectation. Full shifts offer useful nearby alternatives. The visual thesis is a bold UF event poster translated into a dense, accessible scheduling tool: cream paper, black rules, and a single poppy-red accent.

The same navigation exposes a volunteer self-service view. An email lookup shows all active shifts and training status, and supports cancellation. Organizer work lives on a separate sign-in-gated route with roster search, training checkoffs, capacity editing, and day-of attendance.

## Architecture

Vinext renders the public and organizer surfaces on Cloudflare Workers. D1 stores volunteers, signups, training records, and event configuration. Reference event data is idempotently seeded by the data-access layer; migrations remain schema-only. All capacity is derived from confirmed and checked-in signups, and the database uniquely constrains each volunteer/shift pair. Server endpoints validate input, recheck capacity inside a transaction-safe batch, and return clear recoverable errors.

## Rules

- Event dates are Monday January 25 through Wednesday January 27, 2027.
- A volunteer cannot hold the same shift twice.
- Cancelled signups do not consume capacity and may be revived.
- Availability is always `capacity - confirmed/checked-in signups`.
- Shift end time must be after start time and no shift crosses midnight.
- Organizer writes require an authenticated Sites visitor; the first deployment remains owner-private.
- Volunteer information is never rendered in the public schedule.

## Validation

Pure scheduling and validation rules have automated tests. The complete deployment build must succeed. The public route and organizer route must render without blocking errors before publishing.
